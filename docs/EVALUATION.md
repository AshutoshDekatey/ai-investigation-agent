# Evaluation protocol

`evals/platform_cases.json`: 60 cases from 12 scenario templates repeated five times. Data is synthetic, small, and correlated. Outcomes cover confirmed settlement/failure, pending, timeout, duplicate debit, conflicting records, missing IDs, incomplete records, policy versions, missing policy, unavailable tools and adversarial input. The packaged bank fixture excludes gold expected decisions; the engine never loads the benchmark. Transaction/ledger reconciliation includes amount/currency checks. Historical policy retrieval is independently tested with a 2025 date and future dates fail closed.

`payments retrieve-eval` measures natural-language-query Recall@5 (one expected relevant policy) and MRR. Missing-policy cases are excluded from this denominator, not counted as successful retrieval. Binary relevance lacks graded human labels; repeated questions limit generalization. `payments evaluate` also compares expected and actual outcome categories, validates citations against the original approved/date-eligible corpus, checks policy version, tool-call coverage and absence of financial-action tools, and records per-case wall-clock latency. Groundedness is a citation-and-record consistency proxy, not an independent natural-language judge. Recommendation accuracy refers to the categorical recommendation outcome. “Unsafe action violation” is restricted to the action surface/human-review flag; it is not a comprehensive language safety measure.

Actual results: [v0.2 test-encoder report](../reports/v0.2-test-encoder.json). It includes every case and failures. No learned semantic performance, Ragas score, live LLM accuracy or independent human judgment is claimed. No Ragas integration is needed for the current rigorous custom deterministic checks. These checks complement rather than substitute future independent human review.

Live summaries are separately bounded to 1–20 cases with explicit opt-in. Usage comes from the provider; estimated cost uses user-supplied rates. Failed calls retain an error type and unknown cost. Summaries are advisory, not graded by the same model. No live evaluations were run. LangSmith tracing likewise remains optional/unverified.
